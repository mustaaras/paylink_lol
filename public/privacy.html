Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026. Last updated August 24, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how paylink.lol (https://paylink.lol) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit the platform, click a destination link, submit a free link, or pay to boost a ranking. It sits alongside our Terms of Service.

Privacy Summary: We keep our data footprint minimal. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track you across other websites, and we use privacy-first, cookieless analytics.

1. Who is Responsible

The data controller responsible for personal data processed through paylink.lol is httpslabs ("we", "us", "our").

  • Privacy & Support Inquiries: support@httpslabs.com
  • Public Contact: @mustaaras on X.com

2. What We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to operate the leaderboard, process checkout, prevent click fraud, and monitor aggregate performance:

  • Listing & Submission Data: The destination URL you submit, category, title, tagline, price tag, and any public metadata or icon resolved for that destination.
  • Payment Data: Payment card numbers, billing addresses, and security codes are handled securely and directly by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store full payment card details. Stripe provides us with transaction confirmation tokens, order IDs, and payment status to fulfill ranking placements.
  • Email Addresses: If you optionally provide an email address when submitting or boosting a listing, we store it solely to associate your listing, provide transaction receipts, or send outbid status alerts.
  • Outbound Clicks & Rate Limiting: When a user clicks an outbound listing link, we record the listing ID, a timestamp, and a one-way cryptographically hashed IP snippet. This hash is used strictly for bot prevention and click-fraud deduplication, never for user profiling or advertising tracking.
  • Technical & Server Logs: Standard request data (such as user-agent, referrers, and network timestamps) processed by our hosting infrastructure and Cloudflare edge network to route traffic and protect against DDoS attacks.
  • Direct Communications: If you contact us via email for support or listing updates, we retain that correspondence to assist you and maintain operational records.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We believe in a clean, privacy-first web experience:

  • No Tracking / Ad Cookies: We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Cookieless Analytics: Web metrics and real-time visitor counts are measured via Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is fully cookieless and does not track individual visitors across sites.
  • Theme Preferences (Local Storage): Your browser stores your Light / Dark mode preference locally (paylink_theme) in localStorage so your theme persists across page loads.

4. Why We Process Your Data (Legal Bases)

  • Contract Performance: To publish your listing, credit bid boosts, update real-time ranks, and deliver leaderboard services.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain platform security, prevent click manipulation or spam listings, resolve public preview metadata, and monitor system health.
  • Legal & Accounting Compliance: To maintain verifiable tax, dispute, and payment records as mandated by applicable statutory laws.

5. Public Listings & Fair Use Metadata

All leaderboard rankings, cumulative bid totals, titles, descriptions, categories, and destination links displayed on paylink.lol are public by design. Anyone, including search engine crawlers, can view this public directory.

When a URL is submitted, our automated preview fetcher requests the destination page to extract open graph tags and favicons for display.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with reputable, privacy-conscious service providers:

  • Stripe (stripe.com) — Secure payment processing and checkout tokenization.
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com) — Edge DNS, SSL, DDoS mitigation, and cookieless web analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any data brokers or advertisers.

7. Data Retention

  • Public Listings: Retained while active on the leaderboard. If a listing is removed, associated metadata is purged from live indexes.
  • Transaction Records: Kept for the duration required by statutory financial, tax, and anti-fraud regulations.
  • Click & IP Hashes: Short-term rolling retention used solely for aggregate click counters and anti-spam verification.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & Global Privacy)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area, UK, and California), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to or export of personal data held about you.
  • Request correction or complete erasure of your listing or contact information.
  • Object to or restrict specific data processing.

To exercise any privacy rights or request listing removals, email us at support@httpslabs.com.

9. Children's Privacy

paylink.lol is intended strictly for adults and commercial web builders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

10. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect infrastructure or legal updates. The current revision date will always be shown at the top of this document.
