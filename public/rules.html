⚡ Platform Guidelines

Platform Rules & Ranking

paylink.lol is a public, real-time attention leaderboard. There are no ads, no API keys, and no revenue share. You pay to stand above everyone else. Rank is the cumulative bid — nothing else.

⚡ 1. How Ranking Works

Listings can be submitted for free ($0) or boosted with whole US dollars ($1 minimum increment). Bids already on the board keep their amount until they raise or get outranked.

Taking #1 costs at least $1 more than the current top bid. Paying less still places you on the leaderboard at whatever rank that cumulative bid can claim. Equal bids stay in the order they were placed — the older bid maintains the higher rank.

Key Rule: Rank = Cumulative Bid Amount DESC, Created At ASC. Rank is strictly mathematical and transparent in real time.

🚀 2. Boosting & Raising Existing Listings

Enter the same website URL or X @handle again to raise that listing to any target rank. The new payment adds directly to your listing's all-time cumulative bid.

App Store, Play Store, GitHub, and similar platform links are keyed uniquely by their path, so separate projects never share a rank.

🛡️ 3. What You Can List

You may list any legitimate product website, SaaS app, mobile app, developer tool, newsletter, domain portfolio, or an X (@handle) creator profile.

Strictly Prohibited:
  • Pornography, NSFW, or sexual content of any kind.
  • Group chat & invite links (Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Signal, Messenger).
  • Link shorteners (bit.ly, t.co, etc.) — destination URLs must resolve to the real website.
  • Malicious, deceptive, or fraudulent schemes.

Query parameters (affiliate tags, tracking strings, UTM parameters) are automatically cleaned and stripped to ensure clean, direct destination links for visitors.

🏷️ 4. Categories & Moderation

Categories are assigned upon submission or auto-detected. If your product is listed in the wrong category, or you need updates, DM @mustaaras on X to have it changed immediately.

💳 5. Payments & Terms

Your listing is published publicly the instant payment is confirmed. Outbound clicks redirect directly to your clean target URL.

A completed payment claims the rank in real time. Because leaderboard rankings and exposure are delivered immediately, payments are non-refundable.
